Build Where You Live · Funders & Sponsorships

Invest in purpose. Expand access to opportunity.

Build Where You Live (BWYL) is Martell Collins's youth-centered platform helping young people discover their interests, define a direction, and navigate tangible resources and opportunities in Chicago. The goal is not just inspiration. It's a practical next step.

The Approach

From possibility to a pathway

Discover purpose

Through speaking and interactive workshops, youth explore their strengths, interests, identity, leadership and the issues they care about.

Navigate resources

BWYL connects youth to publicly available education, employment, community support and development opportunities, without guaranteeing enrollment or placement.

Experience community action

Programming can introduce youth to agriculture, environmental justice, civic engagement and violence prevention through practical community experiences.

Build sustained opportunity

Support can help expand access to workshops, outreach, youth resource navigation and future program delivery.

Meet the Founder

A conversation with Martell Collins

Martell is a Chicago community advocate, speaker, and founder of Build Where You Live and Wood St. Collective. His work connects youth leadership with neighborhood action, environmental initiatives, community engagement and violence prevention.

A 30-minute introductory conversation can cover his story, the origin of BWYL, the youth it seeks to serve, examples of community work, and ways to collaborate or provide support.

Questions we can explore together

  • What challenges do Chicago youth face in identifying their next opportunity?
  • How does BWYL connect a young person's interests with practical resources?
  • What could a sponsored youth workshop or series look like?
  • What outcomes could we document and report together?
