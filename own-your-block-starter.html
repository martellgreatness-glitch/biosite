What You’ll Work Through

Module 1

Know What You’re Building. Clarify the problem, users, purpose, maintenance plan and vision before pursuing land.

Module 2

Find the Right Lot. Understand ownership types, city-owned land, private parcels and the importance of legal access.

Module 3

Research Before You Spend. Learn the basics of ownership, title, zoning, environmental conditions and physical due diligence.
