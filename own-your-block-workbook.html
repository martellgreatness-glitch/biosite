Worksheet 2

Lot Research Checklist

Do not assume a vacant parcel is available, city-owned, buildable, or appropriate for your use. Document what you can verify.

Chicago Research Resources

ChiBlockBuilder: City-owned vacant lots managed through Chicago's land disposition system. View City-owned lots
Cook County Property Tax Portal: Search by property address or PIN for property, tax, and recorded-document information. Open property search
Chicago Zoning & Land Use Map: Search by address, PIN, or intersection to view zoning and land-use information. Open zoning map

These tools are starting points, not legal determinations. Program rules, lot availability, zoning, environmental conditions, and acquisition requirements can change.
