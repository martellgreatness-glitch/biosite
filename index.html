About Martell

Building where I live — and helping others do the same.

Martell Collins is a Chicago-based community strategist, speaker, and Executive Director of Wood St. Collective. His work is rooted in transforming overlooked spaces, strengthening youth leadership, expanding environmental opportunity, and helping residents navigate the systems shaping their neighborhoods.

Through Build Where You Live, Martell turns lived experience into practical tools, conversations, and strategies for residents and institutions ready to create stronger local impact.
