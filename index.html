About

Community development grounded in lived experience.

Martell Collins is a Chicago-based community strategist, speaker, and Executive Director of Wood St. Collective. His work focuses on transforming overlooked spaces, strengthening youth leadership, and helping residents better understand and navigate the systems shaping their neighborhoods.

His work connects public space, civic engagement, environmental and agricultural justice, entrepreneurship, local ownership, and community investment — making those ideas practical, culturally relevant, and useful.

Through Build Where You Live and his leadership of Wood St. Collective, Martell creates pathways for young people, residents, schools, nonprofits, and community partners to move from ideas to action.
