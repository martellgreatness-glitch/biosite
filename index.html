About

Community development grounded in lived experience.

Martell Collins is a Chicago-based community strategist and founder focused on transforming overlooked spaces, strengthening youth leadership, and helping residents understand how to navigate the systems shaping their neighborhoods.

His work connects public space, civic engagement, environmental and agricultural justice, entrepreneurship, and community investment — making those ideas practical, culturally relevant, and useful.

Through Build Where You Live and his nonprofit work with Wood St. Collective, Martell creates pathways for young people, residents, schools, nonprofits, and community partners to move from ideas to action.
